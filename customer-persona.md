# Customer Persona and Interview Script: B2B Kirana Inventory SaaS

## 1. List of Core Assumptions to Test
1.  Kirana owners find tracking inventory manually to be a major weekly stress factor.
2.  Store owners lose significant sales due to popular items going out of stock unexpectedly.
3.  Shopkeepers are willing to pay a recurring monthly subscription (₹300) for a software tool.
4.  Operators will regularly use a smartphone app during active, busy retail hours.
5.  Users are comfortable typing or using a smartphone camera to scan barcodes for items.

## 2. Neutral Interview Script (10 Open Questions)
*Focusing on past behavior and concrete facts rather than future promises or leading lines.*

1.  How do you currently keep track of items that are running low in your shop?
2.  Can you walk me through the last time you completely ran out of a high-demand product? What did you do?
3.  How do you prepare your inventory order list before speaking with a wholesale distributor salesman?
4.  Tell me about the last time a customer asked for an item that you thought was in stock, but wasn't.
5.  What apps or digital tools do you currently use on your phone to run your daily business?
6.  How much time did you spend checking stock levels or counting products over the past week?
7.  When was the last time you purchased dead stock that didn't end up selling? How did that happen?
8.  How do you currently calculate whether your store made a net profit or loss at the end of the month?
9.  Tell me about the last business app or tool you paid money for. Why did you decide to purchase it?
10. What is the most frustrating part of managing your store inventory during peak evening rush hours?

## 3. Practice Interview Notes (Anonymized)
*Subject:* Shop Owner, Tier-2 City (Grocery retail for 12 years).

### Verified Hard Facts (What actually happened)
*   The store uses a physical cardboard binder notebook ("Khata Book") to manually write down items that run low.
*   Last Tuesday, the store ran completely out of a major brand of cooking oil; it took 4 days for the new distributor delivery to arrive.
*   The owner uses WhatsApp daily to chat with family and uses phone billing via a UPI QR code at the front desk.
*   The owner spent approximately 4 hours closing up the shop late on Sunday night just to visually count boxes on shelves.

### Subjective Opinions & Feelings (What they believe or feel)
*   Believes that software apps are generally too complicated and built only for massive supermarkets.
*   Feels frustrated when wholesale distributors don't show up on time but accepts it as part of ordinary business operations.
*   Expresses a strong desire to save time so they don't have to stay at the shop past 11:00 PM on weekends.

### Surprises
*   The owner was strongly opposed to typing product names into an app, but showed enthusiasm when demonstrating how they already scan UPI barcodes using their smartphone camera.

## 4. Two User Personas (Derived from Interview Evidence)

### Persona A: "The Traditional Optimizer" (Anonymized Profile)
*   **Background:** 42-year-old independent shopkeeper running a family-inherited corner grocery store in a Tier-2 neighborhood. Comfort level with basic apps is high (WhatsApp, YouTube, PhonePe).
*   **Habits:** Logs supplier details in a physical diary. Spends 30 minutes every morning visually scanning shelves while sitting at the counter.
*   **Goals:** Wants to reduce manual manual stock-counting work so they can spend more evenings at home with family.
*   **Core Pains:** Loses steady walk-in customers when household essentials suddenly run out before supplier reorders arrive. 
*   **Real Interview Quote:** *"If I can't see an item on the top shelf during a rush, I don't know it's gone until a customer complains."*

### Persona B: "The Digital Transitioner" (Anonymized Profile)
*   **Background:** 28-year-old assistant operator who took over operational duties of their parents' store. Highly tech-literate.
*   **Habits:** Handles all customer payments via digital UPI codes. Tries to use Excel on an old home PC but struggles to keep entries updated daily.
*   **Goals:** Wants to modernize the store layout, introduce fast checkouts, and eliminate paper-based tracking completely.
*   **Core Pains:** Struggling to convince older family members to adopt heavy accounting software platforms due to interface complexity.
*   **Real Interview Quote:** *"We need something that functions fast like a phone camera scanner, not a ledger accounting course."*
