# Value Proposition Canvas Report: B2B Kirana Inventory SaaS

## 1. Selected Customer Segment Profile
*   **Target Segment:** "The Traditional Optimizer" (Based on Persona A from Project 03).
*   **Customer Profile Description:** Independent, small-scale Kirana store operators in Tier-2 Indian cities who manage stock manually and are highly sensitive to business disruptions.

### Customer Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)
1.  **Functional:** Checking daily stock levels on grocery shelves to determine what needs reordering.
2.  **Functional:** Compiling clear, accurate supplier ordering lists before regional wholesale salesmen arrive.
3.  **Social/Emotional:** Reducing personal anxiety related to turning walk-in neighborhood customers away empty-handed.

### Ranked Customer Pains
1.  **Pain 1 (Rank 1):** Unanticipated stockouts of high-velocity items resulting in immediate revenue loss.
2.  **Pain 2 (Rank 2):** Wasting late-night operational hours manually counting inventory boxes on high shelves.
3.  **Pain 3 (Rank 3):** Fear of technical complexity or complicated keyboard typing workflows during peak customer rush hours.

### Ranked Customer Gains
1.  **Gain 1 (Rank 1):** Immediate visibility into accurate, real-time stock levels directly from a phone screen.
2.  **Gain 2 (Rank 2):** Reclaiming personal weekend personal time to spend with family rather than doing manual audits.
3.  **Gain 3 (Rank 3):** Seamless transaction-handling speeds by tracking item placement effortlessly.

---

## 2. Value Map (Our Product Offer)
*   **Core Product/Service:** Simplified Smartphone-Based Mobile Inventory Companion App (Priced at ₹300/month).

### Pain Relievers
*   **Reliever for Pain 1:** Automated real-time stock low alerts and push notifications when a product reaches a preset minimum inventory threshold.
*   **Reliever for Pain 2:** Zero-configuration smartphone camera barcode scanner module to log item updates in seconds.
*   **Reliever for Pain 3:** Minimalist visual interface with native local regional vernacular language support (e.g., Hindi, Telugu, Marathi).

### Gain Creators
*   **Creator for Gain 1:** One-click dashboard view displaying active item counts sorted by category and brand layout.
*   **Creator for Gain 2:** One-tap export of ordering lists to WhatsApp, saving hours of manual data collation.
*   **Creator for Gain 3:** Full local offline caching, allowing the app to open instantly and record scans even without active internet coverage during rush hours.
