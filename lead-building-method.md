# Project 05: Lead List Building & Prospecting Method Note

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Target
Our ideal targets are independent, active traditional retail grocery points (Kirana stores) based in growing non-metropolitan hubs (Tier-2 locations like Pune, Nashik, Nagpur, Aurangabad, and Solapur) that demonstrate strong local digital footprint markers (e.g., claimed Google Maps listings, active UPI transaction setups, digital store logs) but lack structured B2B inventory analytics tools.

## 2. Prospect Scoring Methodology (Max 15 Points)
Leads are evaluated quantitatively using verified public data parameters on a 1–5 scale across three core vectors:
1.  **Estimated Scale & Size (1 to 5):** Calculated relative to visible shelf space indicators and inventory variety on verified public directory maps. (1 = Micro kiosk, 5 = Large organized mini-supermarket).
2.  **Stockout Pain Propensity (1 to 5):** Scored based on footfall patterns and volume of high-velocity fast-moving items handled. (1 = Low turnover, 5 = High daily turnover).
3.  **Digital/Tech Familiarity (1 to 5):** Based on baseline indicators such as integrated digital payment setups and claimed web profiles. (1 = Strictly cash-only, 5 = Full digital payment onboarding).

### Classification Thresholds:
*   **Total Fit Score 12 to 15:** Categorized as **Priority Tier** (High intent, high digital readiness).
*   **Total Fit Score Below 12:** Categorized as **Standard Tier** (Requires foundational high-touch offline sales demonstration).

## 3. Data Cleaning, Quality, and Privacy Safeguards
*   **Duplicate Control Verification:** A comprehensive distinct subset constraint filter was executed across the company names index, confirming exactly 0 duplicate listings exist.
*   **Strict Anti-Spam & Privacy Alignment:** In compliance with the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, zero private personal contacts or personal consumer emails (e.g., individual Gmail/Yahoo addresses) were gathered or scraped. Every recorded element relies strictly on role-based, publicly exposed commercial directory channels intended explicitly for business communication.
